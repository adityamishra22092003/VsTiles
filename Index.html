Our Philosophy

Where stone becomes sculpture

For two decades, Tile Wale Bhaiya has been Central India's destination for architects, designers and homeowners who refuse to compromise. Our showroom is not a store — it is a gallery of surfaces, curated from Italy, Spain, Morbi and beyond.

Every slab, every fitting, every finish is chosen for one reason: it belongs in a space meant to be remembered.

  • Exclusive imported Italian marble & porcelain
  • In-house design consultation for architects
  • Authorised dealer for 250+ premium global brands
  • End-to-end supply for villas, hotels & residences
